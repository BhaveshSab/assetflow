=== how it is wired ===

Prometheus rule fires → Alertmanager (127.0.0.1:9093) → receiver "telegram"
→ api.telegram.org. The credentials live in infra/.env (mode 600, git-ignored)
and reach the container through env_file; render.sh substitutes them into
alertmanager.yml at container start, so they are never baked into an image.
